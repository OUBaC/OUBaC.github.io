If you are interested in the club, please come visit us at the Freshers' Fair! If you can't make it, then please see below for a general summary of what this club offers and how you can get involved!

  • When: Wed 7th Oct (9.00-17.00)/Thur 8th Oct (9.00-16.00)
  • Where: Examination Schools (Merton St Entrance)

Overview: Clubnight

  • Who Is It For: Anyone! These sessions are open to anybody (although spaces are limited).
  • Where: Sessions are usually at Iffley Sports Centre (OX4 1EQ).
  • When: Varies per week. You need to check the PlayWaze site, which is where all clubnight sessions are listed.
  • Is It Free: No. You can pay per session, or purchase a membership.
  • More Details: For more details on location, signing up for sessions, and memberships, check out the Clubnight page!

Overview: Squad

  • Who Is It For: For those who are interested in competition-level badminton and want to play in BUCS matches and/or local league games.
  • What Is The Process: The process to join the squad is as follows:
    1. Sign-up for trials using the Google form: Here!
    2. Pass open trials.
    3. Pass closed placement trials.
    4. The team that you get placed in (M1/M2/M3/W1/W2) will dictate whether you play BUCS games against other universities or local league games against local clubs.
  • Is It Free: No. Anyone that joins the team needs to pay a membership fee.
  • Is There Cool Merch: Yes!!!
  • More Details: For more details on squad trials, head to the Squad Trials page, and for more details on the squad generally, check out the Squad page!
